# Budgets, Scheduled Bills & Subscription Intelligence

Moneta provides proactive cash flow management with flexible payday budgeting, 14-day bill reminder horizons, and AI-driven recurring subscription detection.

---

## 1. Category Envelope Budgets & Payday Cycles

Unlike rigid calendar-month budgeting tools, Moneta accommodates real-world salary paydays:

* **Custom Payday Start Days**: Set the budget cycle to begin on the 15th, 25th, or last day of the month.
* **Visual Gauges**: Color-coded progress bars indicate spending vs. budget limit:
  - 🟢 **Normal** ($< 80\%$ spent)
  - 🟡 **Warning** ($80\% - 100\%$ spent)
  - 🔴 **Over Budget** ($> 100\%$ spent)

---

## 2. Scheduled Bills & Payment Reminders

Track recurring utilities, rent, credit card dues, and insurance premiums:

* **Flexible Recurrence Frequencies**:
  - Once, Daily, Weekly, Biweekly, Every 4 Weeks, Semimonthly
  - Monthly, Every 2 Months (Bimonthly), Quarterly, Every 4 Months (Triannual)
  - Semiannual (Half-Yearly), Yearly (Annual), Every 2 Years (Biennial)
* **14-Day Outlook Horizon**: Displays upcoming obligations on the dashboard.
* **Badges**: Overdue (🔴), Due Today (🟡), Due in 7 Days (🔵).
* **Multi-Currency Scheduling**: Enter bills in foreign currencies with custom conversion rates.
* **1-Click Execution & Auto-Post**: Click **Post Bill** to generate the actual transaction in the checkbook ledger and advance the next recurrence date, or enable **Auto-post** for hands-free daily cron execution.

---

## 3. Smart Subscription Detector

Moneta scans historical transactions to identify recurring subscription charges:
* Calculates recurrence cadence (weekly, monthly, annual).
* Flags hidden spending leaks and silent price increases.
* Allows 1-click conversion into tracked scheduled bills.
